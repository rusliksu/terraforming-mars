# Explicit source limitations

- Spome policy 4 describes EPIC campaign towns and an individual city track, neither implemented by the source engine. Keep the explicit unavailable description; do not invent the mechanic. The other Spome rules and Moon gating are transferred.
- Great Martian Archives supplies a normal tableau wild tag. The printed claim of counting tags while in hand is explicitly unimplemented in corrected source and is not added by this port.
- Conglomerates retains source team-VP ordering and its existing individual-MC tie handling. No authoritative alternative tie rule was identified; no new rule is claimed.
- The source also adapts EPIC-only Bureaucrats, Populists and Transhumanists effects, and the Mariners Agreements event, into supported base-engine effects. This port preserves those implemented adaptations rather than claiming literal EPIC behavior.
- Custom-card saves written before metadata snapshots cannot recover a deleted definition or a Deimos source already lost by the old name-only format. New saves retain that information.
