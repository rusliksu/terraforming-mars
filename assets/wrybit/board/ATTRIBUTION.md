# Wrybit board artwork

The two clean Mars board images are copied unchanged from
[wrybit/terraforming-mars](https://github.com/wrybit/terraforming-mars/tree/988235122ffd61d8d1190e90beb2247afd252c00/assets/board).
Their original paths and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `src/client/wrybit/import-manifest.json`.

This import retains the source repository's GPL-3.0 distribution and upstream game-art attribution.
The images are loaded by the optional Wrybit presentation.
